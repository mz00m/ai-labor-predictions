# Education & Training — Human Capabilities

## Adaptive Teaching & Real-Time Responsiveness

> Reading a room of learners and adjusting in the moment — speeding up, slowing down, switching approaches when the planned lesson isn't landing.

- **Function**: education
- **Also relevant to**: people-management, communications
- **Why appreciating**: AI tutors can personalize content delivery at scale. They cannot see that the student in the third row is confused but too embarrassed to ask, or that the energy in the room dropped because the example didn't connect. The teacher who reads these signals and adapts — pivoting from lecture to discussion to hands-on activity based on real-time feedback — is doing the work that makes learning actually happen.
- **How to develop**: Record yourself teaching (even just screen recordings of virtual sessions) and review for moments where you missed signals or where a pivot worked. Practice having three versions of every key concept ready: technical, analogy-based, and visual. Build a repertoire of "back pocket" activities for when the plan isn't working. After each session, note: what did students actually learn vs. what I planned to teach?

## Mentorship & Individual Potential Recognition

> Seeing what a student or trainee could become — and investing in their growth with the patience, challenge, and belief that catalyzes transformation.

- **Function**: education
- **Also relevant to**: people-management
- **Why appreciating**: AI can identify learning gaps through assessment data. It cannot see the student who is underperforming because they don't believe they belong, or the one who is bored because the material is too easy. Research shows that AI's effect on boosting demand for human mentorship and interpersonal skills is nearly twice as large as its effect on replacing tasks. The educator who recognizes potential — especially potential that the learner doesn't see in themselves — and finds the right combination of challenge and support to unlock it, is doing the most important work in education.
- **How to develop**: For each student or trainee, write one sentence about what they could achieve that they don't yet believe is possible. Practice the "warm demander" approach: high expectations communicated with genuine care. Study Carol Dweck's growth mindset research — not the pop version, the actual research on how belief about capability affects learning. Build individual check-ins into your practice: 5 minutes of genuine conversation about goals, not just performance.

## Learning Experience Design

> Crafting educational experiences that create genuine understanding — not just information transfer, but the "aha moment" where concepts click.

- **Function**: education
- **Also relevant to**: marketing, creative-design
- **Why appreciating**: AI can generate curriculum, practice problems, and explanations. It cannot design the activity where students discover the principle themselves, or sequence a learning experience so that each concept builds naturally on the last, or create the project that makes abstract knowledge feel urgent and relevant. Learning design is a creative discipline that combines content expertise with deep understanding of how humans actually learn.
- **How to develop**: Study learning science: spaced repetition, interleaving, retrieval practice, desirable difficulties. Design one "discovery-based" activity per unit — where students encounter the problem before the solution. A/B test your approaches: try two different ways to teach the same concept and compare comprehension. Read "Make It Stick" by Brown, Roediger, and McDaniel for evidence-based learning strategies.

## Assessment Integrity & Meaningful Evaluation

> Evaluating learning in ways that resist AI circumvention and actually measure understanding — not just information recall.

- **Function**: education
- **Also relevant to**: people-management, technology
- **Why appreciating**: AI makes traditional assessments obsolete. Any take-home essay, problem set, or coding assignment can be AI-generated. The educator who can design assessments that genuinely measure understanding — oral examinations, live problem-solving, portfolio defense, collaborative projects with individual accountability — is solving one of education's most urgent problems. This capability will define the credibility of educational institutions.
- **How to develop**: Redesign one assessment this term to be AI-resistant: oral component, live demonstration, or portfolio defense. Study authentic assessment frameworks: what does it look like to demonstrate genuine competency? Build rubrics that evaluate thinking process, not just output. Experiment with collaborative assessments where individual contributions are traceable. Accept that some AI use in learning is beneficial — the skill is knowing what to assess AI-free.
