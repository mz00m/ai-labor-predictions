# Marketing & Brand — Human Capabilities

## Brand Intuition & Taste

> The ability to feel whether something is on-brand without checking a style guide — and to evolve the brand without breaking it.

- **Function**: marketing
- **Also relevant to**: creative-design, communications
- **Why appreciating**: AI generates infinite content, but it has no taste. It can match patterns but can't feel when something is trying too hard, or when a subtle shift in tone would land better. The mechanism is now measured: Doshi and Hauser (Science Advances, 2024) found that writers given generative-AI story ideas produced work judged more creative individually, yet markedly more similar to each other — individual quality up, collective distinctiveness down. Follow-on work through 2026 shows that homogenization is a function of how the model is prompted rather than a fixed limit, which puts the burden squarely on the person directing it. One correction to this record: the job-postings study previously cited here (Mäkelä and Stephany, expanded in June 2026 to nearly 30 million postings across the US, UK and Australia) does not find rising employer demand for "taste." The complements it names are analytical thinking, resilience, digital literacy and ethical judgment. Taste is a real advantage, but the evidence for it is a mechanism, not a hiring signal.
- **How to develop**: Build a swipe file of marketing you genuinely admire (not just "best practices" lists). For every piece your team produces, write one sentence about what makes it distinctly yours vs. what any brand could say. Study brands that maintained identity through decades of change (Apple, Patagonia, The Economist). Practice editing AI output: what do you cut, add, or reword? Vary your prompting deliberately — single-persona prompting is what drives outputs toward the median.

## Audience Empathy & Cultural Context

> Understanding what your actual audience cares about, fears, and aspires to — not what the data says they should care about.

- **Function**: marketing
- **Also relevant to**: sales, communications, customer-service
- **Why appreciating**: AI can segment audiences and predict click-through rates. It can't sit in a customer's kitchen and understand why they chose your product over the cheaper alternative. What changed since this record was last verified is that vendors now sell "synthetic respondents" — LLM personas marketed as a substitute for talking to customers. The evidence supports narrow use only. Stanford work (Park et al., 2024) shows an LLM agent can reproduce a specific person's survey answers at roughly 85% of that person's own two-week test-retest reliability. But when synthetic samples stand in for real ones, response variance collapses and roughly half of the statistical relationships in the data shift (Bisbee et al., 2024). Synthetic panels approximate the average customer and erase the edges — and the edges are where positioning is won.
- **How to develop**: Do 5 customer interviews this quarter with no agenda other than understanding their world. Read the forums, subreddits, and comment sections where your audience talks when they don't know you're listening. Build customer journey maps that include emotional states, not just touchpoints. Test your assumptions: write what you think customers want, then validate it. If you use a synthetic panel, restrict it to early concept and copy screening — never to sizing a market or making a final call.

- **Task categories**: interpersonal, analysis-decision
- **Appreciation score**: 9
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

## Strategic Narrative Construction

> Building the story that connects product capabilities to human outcomes — the "why should I care" bridge.

- **Function**: marketing
- **Also relevant to**: communications, sales
- **Why appreciating**: Generation is no longer the constraint, and this record used to claim it was. Csaszar, Ketkar and Kim (Strategy Science, 2024) had 250 experienced investors blind-evaluate strategies from an accelerator and a startup competition; LLM-generated strategies rated comparable to the entrepreneurs' own. A late-2025 preprint on ad copy found LLM output preferred 59% to 41% against human writers, holding up even when readers were told its origin. Two things still constrain. First, choosing which of five plausible narratives the company actually bets on, and being accountable when it is wrong — unsigned strategy is the failure mode, and most executives concede they could not pass an AI governance audit on short notice. Second, distinctiveness: Doshi and Hauser (Science Advances, 2024) show AI-assisted work converges toward the collective median, which is precisely what positioning exists to escape. The value moved from writing the narrative to owning it and refusing the average one.
- **How to develop**: Generate five candidate narratives with a model in an afternoon — that is now the cheap part — then do the work it cannot: pick one, write down why the other four lose, and put your name on the call. Study how the best companies frame themselves (not their products — their role in the world). Practice writing the same product benefit three ways: feature-first, benefit-first, worldview-first. Check any AI-assisted draft against what your three closest competitors are already saying; convergence toward the median is the documented failure mode. Write your company's narrative in 100 words, 50 words, and 10 words.

- **Task categories**: creative-generative, communication, analysis-decision

- **Confidence**: medium

## Channel Judgment & Resource Allocation

> Knowing which channels actually drive value vs. which just generate activity — and having the conviction to say no.

- **Function**: marketing
- **Also relevant to**: operations, finance
- **Why appreciating**: AI makes it trivially cheap to be present on every channel, and measurement is getting worse rather than better at telling you which ones work. Forrester's 2026 B2C marketing predictions expect marketers' confidence in their ability to measure marketing impact to slip about 7% this year, as AI-mediated discovery moves buyer research onto surfaces conventional attribution cannot see. In the same research, 52% of B2C marketing executives expect tighter budgets and 51% expect smaller teams. Cheap production, degraded measurement, and fewer people is precisely the condition where judgment about where not to play carries the most value.
- **How to develop**: Audit your current channels: which ones drive pipeline, which drive vanity metrics? Run a "channel fast" — stop posting on your weakest channel for 30 days and measure the actual business impact. Prefer a holdout or geo test over a modeled attribution number you cannot audit; modeled attribution has degraded fastest exactly where AI-mediated discovery is growing. Practice saying no to new channel opportunities with a one-sentence rationale.

## Cross-Functional Translation

> Translating between engineering, sales, product, and executive language — making marketing the connective tissue of the organization.

- **Function**: marketing
- **Also relevant to**: operations, communications, people-management
- **Why appreciating**: As AI handles more production work, marketing's value shifts from making things to connecting things — and organizations are removing the layers that used to do the connecting. Revelio Labs finds middle-management job postings down roughly 42% from their April 2022 peak, with no recovery through late 2025. Gusto's payroll data shows the average small-business manager going from about three direct reports in 2019 to about six by 2024. The coordination work did not disappear; it concentrated in fewer people. The marketer who can sit in a product roadmap meeting, extract the story, align it with sales messaging, and brief the exec team is absorbing work that used to be spread across several roles — which is leverage, and also a load-management problem worth naming out loud.
- **How to develop**: Attend one meeting per month outside your function (engineering standup, sales pipeline review, customer support retrospective). Build a glossary of how different teams describe the same things differently. Practice translating: take a technical spec and write the customer-facing story in 15 minutes. Use AI for the first draft of the translation, then check it against what you actually heard in the room — the model has the vocabulary but not the context.

- **Task categories**: communication, interpersonal, coordination-management
