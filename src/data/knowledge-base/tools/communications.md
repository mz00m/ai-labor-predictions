# Communications & Public Relations — AI Tools

## Grammarly

- **Function**: communications
- **Also useful for**: administrative, marketing, education
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($12/mo for Pro)
- **URL**: https://www.grammarly.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI writing assistant for grammar, clarity, tone, and style across all writing surfaces.

**Best for:** Professionals who write frequently (emails, reports, proposals). Works as a browser extension, desktop app, and integrates with most writing tools. Tone detection helps match communication style to audience.

**Limitations:** The paid tier is now called Pro, not Premium. Free tier caps generative use at 100 AI prompts. Can be overly prescriptive about style. The AI rewrite suggestions sometimes strip voice/personality from writing.

## Otter.ai

- **Function**: communications
- **Also useful for**: administrative, people-management, sales
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($16.99/user/mo for Pro, $8.33 billed annually)
- **URL**: https://otter.ai
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> Real-time meeting transcription, summarization, and action item extraction.

**Best for:** Professionals in meeting-heavy roles. Auto-joins Zoom/Teams/Meet calls, generates transcripts with speaker identification, extracts action items, and creates shareable summaries.

**Limitations:** Accuracy drops with heavy accents, overlapping speakers, or poor audio. Free Basic tier limited to 300 transcription minutes/month and 5 workspace members. Pro caps meetings at 90 minutes. Requires calendar integration for auto-join.

## Loom

- **Function**: communications
- **Also useful for**: marketing, people-management, education
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($18/user/mo for Business; AI features require Business + AI at $24/user/mo)
- **URL**: https://www.loom.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> Async video messaging with AI-powered summaries, chapters, and transcripts.

**Best for:** Replacing status update meetings with async video. Record screen + camera, AI generates summary and chapters. Viewers can watch at 2x or just read the summary.

**Limitations:** Business jumped from $12.50 to $18/user/mo, and the AI summary and video-to-text features that make Loom worth it are now gated behind the separate Business + AI tier at $24/user/mo. Not a replacement for interactive discussions. Video fatigue is real. Free Starter tier limited to 25 videos and 5 min each.
