# Creative & Design — AI Tools

## Midjourney

- **Function**: creative-design
- **Also useful for**: marketing, communications
- **Pricing**: Paid ($10/mo Basic, $30 Standard, $60 Pro, $120 Mega; 20% off annual)
- **URL**: https://www.midjourney.com/checkout/plans
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> AI image generation with exceptional aesthetic quality, accessed via the web app or Discord.

**Best for:** Concept art, marketing visuals, mood boards, social media imagery. Produces consistently beautiful results with relatively simple prompts. Strong at photorealistic and artistic styles. The web app is now the primary interface — Discord is optional, not required.

**Limitations:** No free tier or trial. Fast GPU hours are the real constraint: 3.3/mo on Basic, 15 on Standard, 30 on Pro, 60 on Mega. Standard and above add unlimited Relax-mode generation; Pro is mainly worth it for Stealth Mode. Limited control over precise details. Not great for text-in-image, technical diagrams, or exact brand specifications. Prices confirmed from multiple independent trackers — the vendor plan page is behind a checkout redirect and is not directly readable.

## Adobe Firefly

- **Function**: creative-design
- **Also useful for**: marketing
- **Pricing**: Standalone $9.99/mo Standard, $19.99 Pro, $49.99 Pro Plus, $199.99 Premium; bundled via Creative Cloud Pro ($69.99/mo)
- **URL**: https://www.adobe.com/products/firefly/plans
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> AI image generation and editing integrated into Photoshop, Illustrator, and Express. Trained on licensed content.

**Best for:** Creative professionals already in the Adobe ecosystem. Generative Fill in Photoshop, text-to-image, style transfer. Commercially safe (trained on Adobe Stock and licensed content).

**Limitations:** The cheap $5 standalone entry point is gone — Firefly Standard now starts at $9.99/mo. Adobe also split all-apps into Creative Cloud Standard ($54.99/mo) and Pro ($69.99/mo), with the full generative feature set only on Pro, and raised Creative Cloud prices 12-25% for 2026. Paid tiers include unlimited standard generations; credits are consumed only by premium features such as video, sound, translation, and partner models. Image quality below Midjourney for artistic work. Prices confirmed from multiple independent trackers; adobe.com blocks automated fetches.

## Suno

- **Function**: creative-design
- **Also useful for**: marketing, communications
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($10/mo for Pro, $8/mo billed annually; $30/mo for Premier)
- **URL**: https://suno.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI music generation from text prompts. Creates full songs with vocals, instruments, and production.

**Best for:** Content creators needing background music, jingles, or podcast intros. Marketing teams creating audio content. Rapid prototyping of musical ideas. Pro adds commercial-use rights, stem separation, and 20 song downloads/month.

**Limitations:** The free tier is not usable for work: no commercial use, no stem separation, and monthly song downloads are being removed from Free as of September 3, 2026. Output quality is impressive but not professional studio quality. Paid plans are metered in credits. Limited control over arrangement details.

## Descript

- **Function**: creative-design
- **Also useful for**: marketing, communications, education
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($16/person/mo Hobbyist, $24 Creator, $50 Business; annual billing)
- **URL**: https://www.descript.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI-powered video and audio editing through text-based editing. Edit video by editing the transcript.

**Best for:** Content creators, podcasters, and marketing teams. Edit video/audio by editing text, remove filler words automatically, generate clips, add captions. Dramatically lowers the skill bar for video production. Creator at $24 is the practical tier for most solo professionals.

**Limitations:** Business more than doubled, from $24 to $50/person/mo — $24 now buys the Creator tier. Monthly billing costs 35% more than the advertised annual rates. Plans are capped in media hours and AI credits (10 hrs/400 credits on Hobbyist). Complex edits still need traditional editors. AI voice cloning requires careful ethical use.
