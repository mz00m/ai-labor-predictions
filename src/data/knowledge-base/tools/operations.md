# Operations & Small Business — AI Tools

## Asana (with AI)

- **Function**: operations
- **Also useful for**: administrative, people-management, marketing
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($10.99/user/mo for Starter, billed annually)
- **URL**: https://asana.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> Project management platform with AI features for status updates, task recommendations, and workflow optimization.

**Best for:** Teams managing complex projects with multiple workstreams. AI generates status reports from task updates, recommends task assignments, and identifies bottlenecks. AI Studio adds workflow agents from the Starter tier up.

**Limitations:** The Premium tier is now called Starter, and the free Personal plan is capped at 2 users — small teams are pushed to paid faster than before. AI Studio runs on metered credits (50K/mo on Starter, 75K on Advanced), so heavy automation has a usage ceiling. Can become bloated for simple projects.

## Automation Anywhere

- **Function**: operations
- **Also useful for**: finance, administrative
- **Pricing**: Enterprise (quote-only; free Community Edition)
- **URL**: https://www.automationanywhere.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> Enterprise automation platform, now positioned around agentic AI rather than RPA alone, for automating repetitive digital tasks across applications.

**Best for:** Large organizations with high-volume, rule-based processes across multiple systems (ERP, CRM, legacy apps). Automates data entry, form filling, cross-system data migration.

**Limitations:** Significant implementation cost and time. Requires dedicated RPA developers. Brittle when underlying applications change their UI. Overkill for small teams. A free Community Edition exists for individuals and students but is machine-capped and not licensed for production use; agentic add-ons (AI Agent Studio, Document Automation) are licensed separately.

## Fieldwire

- **Function**: operations
- **Also useful for**: administrative
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($39/user/mo for Pro, billed annually)
- **URL**: https://www.fieldwire.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> Construction management platform for task tracking, plan markup, and field reporting, now sold as Fieldwire by Hilti.

**Best for:** Construction managers and field supervisors. Digital plan markup, punch lists, daily reports, and photo documentation from job sites. Reduces paperwork and improves communication between field and office.

**Limitations:** The free Basic plan caps at 5 users, 3 projects, and 100 sheets, so most crews outgrow it quickly; $39 is the annual rate and monthly billing runs higher. Adoption requires training field crews on mobile devices. Most valuable for commercial construction, less for small residential jobs.

## Procore

- **Function**: operations
- **Also useful for**: finance
- **Pricing**: Enterprise (quote-only; priced on annual construction volume)
- **URL**: https://www.procore.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> Construction management platform with AI-powered risk prediction, quality tracking, and safety compliance.

**Best for:** Large construction firms managing multiple projects. AI analyzes project data to predict delays, identify safety risks, and flag quality issues before they become problems.

**Limitations:** Priced on annual construction volume rather than seats, so cost scales with the business, not the team — users and storage are unlimited. No published rates; every deal is quoted. Implementation takes months. Overkill for small contractors.

## Augmentir

- **Function**: operations
- **Also useful for**: people-management
- **Pricing**: Enterprise (quote-only)
- **URL**: https://www.augmentir.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> Agentic AI connected worker platform for manufacturing, with digital work instructions, an industrial assistant, and skills management.

**Best for:** Manufacturing and maintenance operations. AI personalizes work instructions based on worker skill level, identifies training gaps, and provides real-time guidance on complex procedures.

**Limitations:** No published pricing or free tier — access starts with a sales conversation. Requires significant upfront investment in deployment and content creation. Most valuable at scale (50+ workers). Needs reliable shop-floor connectivity.

## SafetyCulture (iAuditor)

- **Function**: operations
- **Also useful for**: healthcare, legal
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($24/user/mo for Premium, billed annually; $29 monthly)
- **URL**: https://mitti.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> Inspection and audit platform with AI-powered issue detection, reporting, and compliance tracking. Renamed Mitti in August 2026.

**Best for:** Any role requiring regular inspections (safety, quality, compliance). Digital checklists, photo evidence, automatic report generation. AI suggests corrective actions based on inspection findings.

**Limitations:** The iAuditor name was retired earlier and SafetyCulture itself rebranded to Mitti on 11 August 2026, so search results and internal documentation will lag the name change. Free tier covers 10 users, 5 active templates, and 300 AI credits per seat per month. Template setup requires initial effort; best ROI for teams doing 10+ inspections/week.
