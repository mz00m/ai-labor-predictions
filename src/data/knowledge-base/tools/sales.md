# Sales & Business Development — AI Tools

## Gong

- **Function**: sales
- **Also useful for**: people-management, customer-service
- **Pricing**: Enterprise (custom pricing, ~$100/user/mo)
- **URL**: https://www.gong.io
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: high

> Revenue intelligence platform that records, transcribes, and analyzes customer calls and meetings.

**Best for:** Sales teams and account managers. AI analyzes conversation patterns, identifies winning behaviors, flags risk signals in deals, and provides coaching recommendations based on actual call data.

**Limitations:** Expensive. Requires buy-in from the entire sales org. Privacy concerns in some jurisdictions require disclosure. Most valuable with 10+ reps.

## Crystal

- **Function**: sales
- **Also useful for**: people-management, communications
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($49/mo for Premium)
- **URL**: https://www.crystalknows.com
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: medium

> AI personality assessment tool that predicts communication styles and provides tailored messaging suggestions.

**Best for:** Sales, recruiting, and relationship management. Analyzes LinkedIn profiles and other public data to predict DISC personality type. Suggests how to write emails, conduct meetings, and negotiate with each person.

**Limitations:** Personality predictions from public data have inherent accuracy limits. Can feel intrusive. Best as a preparation aid, not a substitute for genuine relationship skills.

## Lavender

- **Function**: sales
- **Also useful for**: communications, marketing
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($29/mo for Pro)
- **URL**: https://www.lavender.ai
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: medium

> AI email coaching for sales outreach. Scores emails in real-time and suggests improvements for response rates.

**Best for:** Sales development reps and anyone doing cold outreach. Analyzes email tone, length, readability, and personalization. Provides a score and specific suggestions before you send.

**Limitations:** Optimizes for response rate, which isn't always the right metric. Can encourage formulaic writing. Most useful for high-volume outreach, less for relationship-based selling.

## Fireflies.ai

- **Function**: sales
- **Also useful for**: people-management, operations, administrative
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($18/seat/mo for Pro)
- **URL**: https://fireflies.ai
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: high

> Meeting assistant that records, transcribes, and generates summaries with action items and sentiment analysis.

**Best for:** Client-facing professionals who need to be fully present in conversations instead of taking notes. Auto-captures everything, generates searchable transcripts, and tracks action items across meetings.

**Limitations:** Requires participant consent for recording. Accuracy varies with audio quality. Sentiment analysis should be treated as directional, not definitive.
