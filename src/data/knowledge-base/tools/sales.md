# Sales & Business Development — AI Tools

## Gong

- **Function**: sales
- **Also useful for**: people-management, customer-service
- **Pricing**: Enterprise (quote only; per-user licenses plus a platform fee)
- **URL**: https://www.gong.io
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> Revenue intelligence platform that records, transcribes, and analyzes customer calls and meetings.

**Best for:** Sales teams and account managers. AI analyzes conversation patterns, identifies winning behaviors, flags risk signals in deals, and provides coaching recommendations based on actual call data. Integrations with an existing tech stack are included at no extra cost.

**Limitations:** Expensive, and Gong publishes no list price — you get a custom proposal sized to headcount, with a separate platform fee on top of per-seat licenses, so budget on total contract value rather than a per-user figure. Requires buy-in from the entire sales org. Privacy concerns in some jurisdictions require disclosure. Most valuable with 10+ reps.

## Crystal

- **Function**: sales
- **Also useful for**: people-management, communications
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($49/mo Premium; Sales from $59/mo)
- **URL**: https://www.crystalknows.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI personality assessment tool that predicts communication styles and provides tailored messaging suggestions.

**Best for:** Sales, recruiting, and relationship management. Analyzes LinkedIn profiles and other public data to predict DISC personality type. Suggests how to write emails, conduct meetings, and negotiate with each person. Your own profile is free permanently, and the sales tier includes three prospect profiles free to trial.

**Limitations:** Personality predictions from public data have inherent accuracy limits. Can feel intrusive. Best as a preparation aid, not a substitute for genuine relationship skills. Pricing is now split by product rather than one subscription: $49/mo Premium covers your own guides and relationship reads, while prospect profiles for selling are a separate $59/mo tier metered at 20 profiles per month. Team assessment reporting starts at $99/mo.

## Lavender

- **Function**: sales
- **Also useful for**: communications, marketing
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($29/mo Starter, $49/mo Pro, $99/seat/mo Team)
- **URL**: https://www.lavender.ai
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI email coaching for sales outreach. Scores emails in real-time and suggests improvements for response rates.

**Best for:** Sales development reps and anyone doing cold outreach. Analyzes email tone, length, readability, and personalization. Provides a score and specific suggestions before you send. Works inside Gmail and Outlook and alongside Outreach, Salesloft, HubSpot, and Apollo.

**Limitations:** Optimizes for response rate, which isn't always the right metric. Can encourage formulaic writing. Most useful for high-volume outreach, less for relationship-based selling. The free tier is now capped at 5 analyzed and 5 personalized emails per month, so it is a trial rather than a usable free plan, and integrations require the $49/mo Pro tier — the $29/mo tier is Starter, not Pro. Annual billing cuts each tier by roughly 10%. Lavender has also added Ora, a separate AI agent that drafts full emails rather than coaching yours.

## Fireflies.ai

- **Function**: sales
- **Also useful for**: people-management, operations, administrative
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($18/seat/mo Pro, $10 billed annually)
- **URL**: https://fireflies.ai
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> Meeting assistant that records, transcribes, and generates summaries with action items and sentiment analysis.

**Best for:** Client-facing professionals who need to be fully present in conversations instead of taking notes. Auto-captures everything, generates searchable transcripts, and tracks action items across meetings. The free tier includes unlimited transcription and unlimited AI summaries, which makes it genuinely usable at low volume.

**Limitations:** Requires participant consent for recording. Accuracy varies with audio quality. Sentiment analysis should be treated as directional, not definitive. The constraints are storage and AI credits, not transcript count: the free tier holds only 400 minutes per team, and AskFred plus the other AI features draw on a monthly credit allowance (20 on Free and Pro, 30 on Business) that pauses those features once exhausted. Monthly billing costs roughly 44% more than annual.
