# Healthcare & Medical Practice — AI Tools

## Nuance DAX Copilot

- **Function**: healthcare
- **Also useful for**: administrative
- **Pricing**: Paid (enterprise/per-provider pricing, typically negotiated per health system)
- **URL**: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/nuance/dax-copilot
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: high

> Ambient clinical documentation that listens to patient-clinician conversations in real time and generates structured clinical notes automatically.

**Best for:** Health systems and large practices looking to reduce clinician documentation burden. DAX Copilot captures the natural patient-provider conversation and drafts encounter notes directly into the EHR, letting physicians review and sign off rather than type from scratch. Particularly strong in primary care and specialty visits with high note volume.

**Limitations:** Requires Microsoft/Nuance enterprise agreement — no self-serve signup for individual providers. Integration depends on EHR compatibility (strongest with Epic and HIPAA-compliant Microsoft cloud infrastructure). Clinicians still must review every generated note for accuracy before signing, which means time savings vary by specialty and documentation complexity.

## Nabla

- **Function**: healthcare
- **Also useful for**: administrative, communications
- **Pricing**: Paid (~$150/provider/mo)
- **URL**: https://www.nabla.com
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: medium

> AI copilot for clinicians that auto-generates visit notes, patient summaries, and follow-up communications from recorded conversations.

**Best for:** Small to mid-size practices and telehealth providers who need ambient documentation without committing to an enterprise platform. Nabla handles visit note generation, after-visit summaries for patients, and drafts of patient messages — reducing administrative time per encounter. Works well for providers doing a mix of in-person and virtual visits.

**Limitations:** Smaller vendor with less EHR integration depth than Nuance or Elation. Note quality can vary for highly specialized medical terminology outside common primary care and behavioral health contexts. HIPAA-compliant, but providers should verify BAA coverage and data handling policies match their compliance requirements.

## Freed

- **Function**: healthcare
- **Also useful for**: administrative
- **Pricing**: Paid ($99/mo per clinician)
- **URL**: https://www.getfreed.ai
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: medium

> AI medical scribe designed for independent practitioners and small practices, generating SOAP notes from patient conversations.

**Best for:** Solo practitioners and small practices that cannot afford a human scribe or an enterprise ambient documentation system. Freed listens to encounters and produces structured SOAP notes that clinicians can edit and finalize. The lower price point and straightforward onboarding make it accessible for practices without dedicated IT staff.

**Limitations:** Less customizable than enterprise solutions — note templates and specialty-specific formatting may not match every workflow. Integration with EHR systems is more limited than Nuance or Elation; many users copy-paste notes rather than having them flow directly into the chart. As with all clinical AI scribes, every note requires physician review to catch errors, and accuracy drops in noisy environments or with heavy medical jargon.

## Elation Health

- **Function**: healthcare
- **Also useful for**: administrative, operations
- **Pricing**: Paid (~$300-500/provider/mo)
- **URL**: https://www.elationhealth.com
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: medium

> EHR platform with built-in AI clinical assistant for documentation, coding suggestions, and patient messaging.

**Best for:** Primary care and direct primary care practices that want AI capabilities embedded natively in their electronic health record rather than bolted on. Elation's AI assists with clinical note drafting, suggests appropriate billing codes based on documentation, and helps generate patient-facing messages — all within the same system clinicians use for charting and ordering.

**Limitations:** Primarily serves primary care; specialists with complex workflow requirements may find it limiting compared to specialty-specific EHRs. The all-in-one approach means switching to Elation requires an EHR migration, which is a significant lift for established practices. AI coding suggestions are assistive only — providers must verify code accuracy to avoid compliance and billing risks.

## Regard

- **Function**: healthcare
- **Also useful for**: operations
- **Pricing**: Paid (enterprise/per-provider pricing)
- **URL**: https://www.regard.com
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: medium

> AI diagnostic assistant that automatically reviews patient charts and surfaces potential diagnoses clinicians may have overlooked.

**Best for:** Hospital medicine and inpatient settings where diagnostic completeness directly affects patient outcomes and accurate clinical documentation. Regard runs in the background, analyzing labs, vitals, medications, and notes to flag conditions that may be present but undocumented — helping hospitalists catch diagnoses that might otherwise be missed during high-volume shifts.

**Limitations:** Focused on inpatient and hospital medicine use cases; not designed for outpatient primary care or specialty workflows. Acts as a clinical decision support tool, not a replacement for clinical judgment — all suggestions require physician evaluation. Enterprise-only sales process means it is not accessible to small practices or individual providers. Effectiveness depends on the quality and completeness of existing chart data.

## Phreesia

- **Function**: healthcare
- **Also useful for**: administrative, customer-service
- **Pricing**: Paid (per-visit pricing, typically negotiated by practice volume)
- **URL**: https://www.phreesia.com
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: high

> Patient intake automation platform with AI-powered scheduling, digital check-in, insurance verification, and pre-visit workflows.

**Best for:** Medical practices and health systems looking to reduce front-desk administrative burden and improve the patient arrival experience. Phreesia handles digital check-in, consent forms, copay collection, real-time insurance eligibility verification, and appointment scheduling — replacing manual intake processes that typically consume significant staff time per patient visit.

**Limitations:** Primarily addresses the administrative and operational side of healthcare, not clinical workflows. Per-visit pricing can add up for high-volume practices, and the ROI depends on how much manual intake work it actually displaces. Customization of intake forms and workflows requires upfront configuration effort, and patient adoption varies — some demographics still prefer paper-based check-in.
