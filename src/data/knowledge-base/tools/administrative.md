# Administrative & Office Support — AI Tools

## ChatGPT

- **Function**: administrative
- **Also useful for**: marketing, sales, finance, communications, technology, education
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($8/mo Go, $20/mo Plus, $100 or $200/mo Pro)
- **URL**: https://chatgpt.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> General-purpose AI assistant for text processing, summarization, data extraction, and Q&A.

**Best for:** Ad-hoc information lookup, document summarization, data extraction from unstructured text. Works across virtually all knowledge work tasks. The $8/mo Go tier sits between Free and Plus for light daily users; Plus is the tier most knowledge workers need, adding the thinking models and the Codex coding agent.

**Limitations:** Can hallucinate facts. Free and Go tiers run smaller models with lower file-upload, image and deep-research allowances. Pro is now split into two price points ($100 and $200/mo) with different usage allowances, so confirm which one you are buying.

## Claude

- **Function**: administrative
- **Also useful for**: technology, finance, legal, communications, education
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($20/mo for Pro, $17/mo billed annually)
- **URL**: https://claude.ai
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI assistant with strong document analysis, long-context processing, and careful reasoning.

**Best for:** Analyzing long documents, careful data extraction, tasks requiring nuanced judgment. Particularly strong at following complex instructions. Paid tiers add Claude Code and the Cowork, Design, and Science surfaces.

**Limitations:** No image generation. Free tier caps usage tightly; heavy users need Max ($100+/mo). Slower than some alternatives for simple tasks.

## Microsoft Copilot (M365)

- **Function**: administrative
- **Also useful for**: communications, finance, operations
- **Pricing**: Paid ($30/user/mo enterprise add-on; $21/user/mo for orgs under 300 seats)
- **URL**: https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-copilot
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI assistant embedded in Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, and Teams.

**Best for:** Workers already in the Microsoft ecosystem. Automates document drafting, email triage, meeting summaries, Excel formula generation, and PowerPoint creation from within the tools people already use. Small orgs now get a cheaper SMB tier, and Microsoft sells Business Standard/Premium bundles with Copilot included.

**Limitations:** Requires a qualifying M365 subscription underneath. Quality varies by application (strong in Word/Outlook, weaker in Excel for complex analysis). Enterprise rollout requires admin configuration. The advertised SMB rate is a promotional discount that expires.

## Google Gemini (Workspace)

- **Function**: administrative
- **Also useful for**: communications, finance, operations
- **Pricing**: Included with Workspace ($7/user/mo Starter, limited AI; $14/user/mo Standard for full Gemini)
- **URL**: https://workspace.google.com/solutions/ai/
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI assistant embedded in Gmail, Docs, Sheets, Slides, and Meet.

**Best for:** Google Workspace users. Email drafting, document summarization, spreadsheet formulas, presentation creation, meeting notes. Gemini is now bundled into every Workspace tier rather than sold as a separate add-on.

**Limitations:** Tightly coupled to Google ecosystem. Starter only includes limited Gemini — full in-app AI starts at Business Standard. Less capable than standalone models for complex reasoning.

## Notion AI

- **Function**: administrative
- **Also useful for**: operations, communications, people-management
- **Pricing**: Bundled into Notion Business ($20/member/mo); trial-only AI on Free and Plus
- **URL**: https://www.notion.com/product/ai
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI features built into Notion's workspace: summarize pages, extract action items, generate content, answer questions across your workspace.

**Best for:** Teams already using Notion for documentation. AI can search across your entire workspace to answer questions, summarize meeting notes, draft project briefs, and extract insights from existing docs.

**Limitations:** Only useful if your team's knowledge is in Notion. Quality depends heavily on how well-organized your workspace is. The old $10/member AI add-on is retired for new customers — reaching full AI now means upgrading to the $20 Business plan, doubling the per-seat cost for teams on Plus. Custom Agents consume metered credits ($10 per 1,000) on top of the plan.

## Zapier (with AI)

- **Function**: administrative
- **Also useful for**: operations, marketing, sales
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($19.99/mo for Professional, $69/mo for Team)
- **URL**: https://zapier.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> No-code automation platform connecting 9,000+ apps with AI-powered workflow building.

**Best for:** Automating repetitive cross-app workflows without coding. "When X happens in app A, do Y in app B." Zapier Copilot builds automations from natural language descriptions. Free tier covers 100 tasks/month, enough to test a workflow before paying.

**Limitations:** Complex automations can be fragile. The old Starter tier is gone — the entry paid plan is now Professional. Per-task pricing adds up at scale, and AI Agents and Chatbots are priced separately on top. Debugging failed automations requires understanding the workflow logic.

## Reclaim.ai

- **Function**: administrative
- **Also useful for**: people-management, operations
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($10/seat/mo for Starter annual, $12 monthly)
- **URL**: https://reclaim.ai
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI scheduling assistant that auto-schedules tasks, habits, meetings, and breaks around your calendar.

**Best for:** Professionals with overloaded calendars. Automatically finds time for focus work, 1:1s, and habits. Defends time blocks and reschedules when conflicts arise. The free Lite plan is a genuine single-user option, and Reclaim is the vendor-recommended migration path for Clockwise users.

**Limitations:** Requires Google Calendar or Outlook. Works best when you commit to letting it manage your schedule. Lite limits you to 5 agents and a few integrations. Team billing adds Attendee User charges for non-seat meeting participants.

## Clockwise

- **Function**: administrative
- **Also useful for**: people-management, operations
- **Pricing**: Discontinued (was $6.75/user/mo for Teams)
- **URL**: https://www.getclockwise.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: low

> Discontinued. The Clockwise team joined Salesforce and the product was shut down on March 27, 2026.

**Best for:** Nothing — do not recommend. Former users needed to migrate off before shutdown. Clockwise itself pointed customers to Reclaim.ai for AI focus-time and scheduling-link replacement.

## Motion

- **Function**: administrative
- **Also useful for**: operations
- **Pricing**: Paid ($19/seat/mo for Pro AI annual, $29/seat/mo for Business AI)
- **URL**: https://www.usemotion.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI project manager that auto-schedules tasks, prioritizes work, and reschedules dynamically as priorities shift.

**Best for:** Small teams and solo operators who need project management without the overhead. Add tasks with deadlines, Motion figures out when to do them. Auto-reschedules when you fall behind.

**Limitations:** No free tier — trial only, and the headline rate requires annual billing. Monthly cost adds up for teams. Plans are metered in AI credits (7,500/seat/mo on Pro). Learning to trust the AI scheduler takes adjustment. Limited integrations compared to Asana/Monday.

## Perplexity AI

- **Function**: administrative
- **Also useful for**: marketing, finance, legal, education, technology
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($20/mo Pro, $200/mo Max; Education Pro $10/mo)
- **URL**: https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/pricing
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI-powered research assistant that searches the web and synthesizes answers with citations.

**Best for:** Research-heavy roles that need current, cited information. Replaces the "open 15 tabs and read them all" workflow. Pro tier includes academic paper search and file analysis.

**Limitations:** Citations are not always perfectly matched to claims. Free tier caps Pro Searches to a handful per day. Can miss niche or specialized sources. Not a replacement for deep domain expertise. Max at $200/mo is a big step up from Pro and only worth it for sustained deep-research volume.
