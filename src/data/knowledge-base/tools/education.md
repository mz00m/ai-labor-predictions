# Education — AI Tools

## Khan Academy Khanmigo

- **Function**: education
- **Also useful for**: people-management
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($44/year for individuals; free for students in participating districts)
- **URL**: https://www.khanacademy.org/khan-labs
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: high

> GPT-4-powered AI tutor and teaching assistant that guides students through problems with Socratic questioning rather than giving answers directly.

**Best for:** K-12 and early college students who need on-demand tutoring in math, science, and humanities. Teachers can use it to generate lesson plans, differentiate instruction, and get real-time insight into where students are struggling. The Socratic approach means students build understanding rather than copying answers.

**Limitations:** Effectiveness depends on students engaging honestly rather than trying to extract answers. Coverage is strongest in math and science — humanities tutoring is less developed. District-wide free access requires institutional partnerships, so individual teachers may need to pay or advocate for adoption.

## MagicSchool AI

- **Function**: education
- **Also useful for**: administrative, communications
- **Pricing**: Freemium (free tier available, $9.99/mo for Plus)
- **URL**: https://www.magicschool.ai
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: high

> AI platform built specifically for teachers with 60+ tools: lesson plan generator, rubric creator, IEP assistant, email drafter, accommodation suggester, and more.

**Best for:** K-12 teachers drowning in planning and paperwork. Generates standards-aligned lesson plans in minutes, drafts IEP goals and accommodations, creates differentiated materials for multiple reading levels, and writes parent communication emails. The tool library is organized around actual teacher workflows rather than generic AI prompting.

**Limitations:** Generated lesson plans still need teacher review and customization — they're solid starting points, not finished products. IEP suggestions must be reviewed by qualified special education staff; relying on AI-generated goals without professional judgment creates compliance and ethical risks. Quality varies across subject areas and grade levels.

## Gradescope

- **Function**: education
- **Also useful for**: administrative
- **Pricing**: Paid (institutional pricing, contact for quote)
- **URL**: https://www.gradescope.com
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: high

> AI-assisted grading platform (owned by Turnitin) that handles assignments, exams, and homework — including handwritten work and code submissions.

**Best for:** Higher education instructors and TAs grading at scale. Upload scanned exams or homework, and AI groups similar answers together so you grade one rubric item across all students at once rather than grading each paper start-to-finish. Handles handwritten math, code autograding (Python, Java, C++, R), and bubble sheets. Cuts grading time by 50-70% in large lecture courses.

**Limitations:** Requires institutional subscription — not available to individual teachers. Handwriting recognition struggles with very messy writing. Initial rubric setup takes effort; the time savings come on subsequent assignments once rubrics are dialed in. Code autograding requires instructor-written test cases.

## Brisk Teaching

- **Function**: education
- **Also useful for**: administrative, communications
- **Pricing**: Freemium (free tier, $9.99/mo for Pro)
- **URL**: https://www.briskteaching.com
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: medium

> Chrome extension AI assistant that works inside the tools teachers already use — Google Docs, Slides, YouTube, and LMS platforms — to provide feedback, create materials, and adjust reading levels.

**Best for:** Teachers who want AI assistance without switching to a separate platform. Highlight student text in Google Docs and get instant feedback suggestions. Generate quiz questions from a YouTube video. Adjust the reading level of a passage while keeping the content intact. The Chrome extension approach means it meets teachers where they already work.

**Limitations:** Chrome-only, so educators on other browsers or school-issued devices with extension restrictions are out of luck. The free tier is limited in daily uses. As a newer entrant, the tool set is narrower than MagicSchool's and the company's long-term trajectory is less established.

## Quillbot

- **Function**: education
- **Also useful for**: communications, administrative
- **Pricing**: Freemium (free tier, $9.95/mo for Premium)
- **URL**: https://www.quillbot.com
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: high

> AI writing assistant specializing in paraphrasing, grammar checking, summarizing, and citation generation — widely used by both students and educators.

**Best for:** Students improving academic writing and non-native English speakers refining their work. The paraphraser helps rephrase sentences while preserving meaning. The citation generator supports APA, MLA, and Chicago formats. Summarizer can condense long readings for study prep. Faculty use it to clean up grant applications, course descriptions, and committee reports.

**Limitations:** The paraphrasing tool is frequently used by students to evade plagiarism detection, which creates academic integrity concerns. The free tier limits paraphrasing to 125 words at a time. Citation generation sometimes produces formatting errors in edge cases — always verify against the style guide. Does not help with the substance of writing, only the surface.

## Synthesia

- **Function**: education
- **Also useful for**: marketing, communications
- **Pricing**: Paid ($22/mo for Starter)
- **URL**: https://www.synthesia.io
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: medium

> AI video generation platform that creates professional training and educational videos using AI avatars — no camera, studio, or video editing skills required.

**Best for:** Higher ed instructional designers and corporate training teams who need to produce video content at scale. Create lecture snippets, orientation videos, compliance training, or multilingual course materials by typing a script and selecting an avatar. Supports 120+ languages, making it useful for institutions serving diverse student populations. Updates are instant — change the script and regenerate rather than re-filming.

**Limitations:** AI avatars are visibly synthetic, which can feel impersonal for relationship-dependent teaching contexts. Not a replacement for an engaging instructor on camera — works better for informational content than pedagogical interaction. The Starter plan limits video length and avatar choices. Students may disengage from avatar-presented content faster than from real instructor video.
