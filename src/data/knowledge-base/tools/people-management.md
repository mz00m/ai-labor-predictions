# People Management & HR — AI Tools

## Lattice

- **Function**: people-management
- **Also useful for**: operations, administrative
- **Pricing**: Paid (modular, $4-13/seat/mo; $4,000/yr minimum)
- **URL**: https://lattice.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> Performance management platform with AI-assisted reviews, OKR tracking, engagement surveys, and compensation benchmarking.

**Best for:** Mid-size and large teams that need a unified system for performance cycles, goal alignment, and employee engagement. The AI features help managers draft review summaries and surface engagement trends across teams. Strong integration with HRIS and Slack. Pricing is now unbundled by module — Engagement at $4/seat/mo, Goals & OKRs at $8, Performance at $10, or the Foundations bundle at $13 — so you can buy only the cycle you actually run.

**Limitations:** Pricing adds up fast at scale since it's per-person, and add-ons like Compensation (+$6/seat/mo) and Grow (+$4) stack on top of a base product. The $4,000 annual minimum spend effectively prices out teams under roughly 25 people. AI-generated review language still needs manager editing to avoid generic phrasing.

## Leapsome

- **Function**: people-management
- **Also useful for**: operations, education
- **Pricing**: Paid (quote only; from $199/mo, modular per-user)
- **URL**: https://www.leapsome.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> People enablement platform combining performance reviews, goal management, engagement surveys, learning paths, and 360-degree feedback.

**Best for:** Organizations that want reviews, goals, engagement, and learning in a single platform rather than stitching together point solutions. European-headquartered with strong GDPR compliance and ISO 27001 certification, which matters for international teams. The learning module ties development plans directly to review outcomes. Now bundles an HRIS layer plus AI agents and workflow automation alongside the talent modules.

**Limitations:** Less name recognition than Lattice or Culture Amp in the US market, which can slow internal buy-in. Reporting and analytics are functional but not as deep as dedicated analytics platforms. Per-module rates are not published — the pricing page is a quote calculator with a stated floor of $199/mo, and onboarding support from the customer success team requires an annual contract of $6,000 or more.

## Textio

- **Function**: people-management
- **Also useful for**: communications, administrative
- **Pricing**: Paid (quote only; free trial of Textio Feedback available)
- **URL**: https://textio.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> AI writing platform that analyzes and improves job postings, performance feedback, and workplace communications for clarity, inclusivity, and bias reduction.

**Best for:** HR teams and hiring managers who want to reduce bias in job descriptions and performance reviews. Textio scores language in real time and suggests alternatives that broaden candidate pools and improve feedback quality. Now sold as two modules — Textio Feedback for performance and interview feedback, Textio Recruiting for hiring communications — usable either in the browser or embedded in your ATS or performance tool.

**Limitations:** No published pricing; every deal runs through a demo and a quote, which puts it out of reach for most small companies. There is now a free trial of Textio Feedback, so evaluating it no longer requires a contract. Effectiveness depends on consistent adoption across managers — one team using it doesn't move org-wide metrics. The bias detection is pattern-based and won't catch all forms of exclusionary language.

## BambooHR

- **Function**: people-management
- **Also useful for**: administrative, operations
- **Pricing**: Paid ($10-25/employee/mo; $250/mo flat under 25 employees)
- **URL**: https://www.bamboohr.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> HR management platform covering employee records, hiring, onboarding, time-off tracking, and reporting with AI-powered workforce analytics.

**Best for:** Small to mid-size businesses that need a core HRIS with built-in hiring and onboarding workflows. Clean interface that non-technical HR staff can manage without training. The Ask BambooHR assistant answers everyday policy and PTO questions against your own company data, with deeper handbook and benefits coverage on Pro.

**Limitations:** There is no free tier — plans run $10/employee/mo for Core, $17 for Pro, and $25 for Elite, and teams of 25 or fewer pay a flat rate starting at $250/mo, which is the effective price floor. Grows less capable at enterprise scale — companies over 500 employees often outgrow it. Payroll and benefits administration are add-ons and not as robust as dedicated payroll providers. Compensation tools and benchmarking require the Elite tier.

## Paradox (Olivia)

- **Function**: people-management
- **Also useful for**: customer-service, administrative
- **Pricing**: Paid (quote only; no published pricing page)
- **URL**: https://www.paradox.ai
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> Conversational AI assistant for recruiting that handles candidate screening, interview scheduling, FAQ responses, and application workflows via text and chat.

**Best for:** High-volume hiring environments — retail, hospitality, healthcare, logistics — where scheduling and screening hundreds of candidates manually is a bottleneck. Olivia handles the back-and-forth of interview coordination and basic qualification checks via SMS or web chat, freeing recruiters for higher-value conversations. Runs either as a standalone conversational ATS or as an agent layer over Workday, SAP SuccessFactors, or Indeed without replacing your system of record.

**Limitations:** Enterprise-only, quote-based pricing with no published rates, which makes it inaccessible for smaller employers and hard to budget without a sales conversation. Works best for structured, high-volume roles; less effective for specialized or executive searches where nuance matters. Candidates occasionally find chatbot interactions impersonal, which can affect employer brand for roles where candidate experience is a differentiator. Paradox now markets the product primarily by capability rather than under the Olivia name, though Olivia remains the assistant.

## Culture Amp

- **Function**: people-management
- **Also useful for**: operations, administrative
- **Pricing**: Paid (quote only; billed annually, varies by headcount and modules)
- **URL**: https://www.cultureamp.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> Employee experience platform with AI-driven engagement surveys, performance management, and development analytics backed by organizational psychology research.

**Best for:** Organizations that take engagement measurement seriously and want science-backed survey design with strong benchmarking data. The analytics layer surfaces drivers of engagement and attrition risk with enough statistical rigor to present to leadership. Sold as two modules, Engage and Perform, with AI Coach included in every plan and a People Science consulting add-on for teams that need help acting on results.

**Limitations:** Culture Amp no longer publishes per-person rates — every tier is request-a-quote, priced on headcount, product mix, and service tier, and billed annually only, so the earlier $5-11/person/mo figure is no longer verifiable. The platform's strength in analytics means it's heavier to administer than simpler survey tools. Getting value requires someone who can interpret the data and act on it — the tool surfaces insights but doesn't implement change. Survey fatigue is a real risk if the organization doesn't close the loop on results.
