# Customer Service & Support — AI Tools

## Intercom Fin

- **Function**: customer-service
- **Also useful for**: sales, marketing
- **Pricing**: Paid ($0.99/Fin outcome, plus $29+/seat/mo for Intercom plans)
- **URL**: https://www.intercom.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI customer service agent that resolves support queries autonomously using your help center content, past conversations, and knowledge base.

**Best for:** Teams already on Intercom who want to deflect repetitive support tickets. Fin reads your help center and resolves straightforward queries without human involvement. Fin can also run standalone on an existing helpdesk such as Salesforce with no seat costs, which is the cheaper path if you are not migrating to Intercom.

**Limitations:** Requires a well-maintained knowledge base to perform well — garbage in, garbage out. Intercom now bills "outcomes" rather than "resolutions," and an outcome counts even when the customer simply stops replying or Fin hands off, so it is broader than a confirmed fix. Seats are billed separately at $29 (Essential) to $132 (Expert) per seat/mo, and the standalone option carries a minimum monthly commitment. Complex or emotionally sensitive issues still need human handoff.

## Zendesk AI

- **Function**: customer-service
- **Also useful for**: operations, administrative
- **Pricing**: Paid ($55/agent/mo for Suite Team, $115/agent/mo for Suite Professional; annual)
- **URL**: https://www.zendesk.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI-powered ticket routing, response suggestions, and customer intent detection built natively into the Zendesk support platform.

**Best for:** Mid-size to enterprise support teams already using Zendesk. AI Agents, AI Knowledge Base, and AI Action Builder start at Suite Team ($55/agent/mo), which is the realistic AI entry point. Strongest when you have years of ticket history for the models to learn from.

**Limitations:** Expensive, and the entry price for AI is higher than commonly quoted — $55/agent/mo buys Suite Team, while Suite Professional is $115/agent/mo. The Copilot agent-assist add-on is a further $50/agent/mo. Support Team at $19/agent/mo includes no AI. Smaller teams may not generate enough ticket volume to justify the cost. AI suggestions require agent review and can be off-base for niche or technical products. Migration away from Zendesk is painful once you're locked in.

## Freshdesk (Freddy AI)

- **Function**: customer-service
- **Also useful for**: operations, administrative
- **Pricing**: Paid ($19/agent/mo for Growth, $55 Pro, $89 Enterprise; annual). No free tier
- **URL**: https://www.freshworks.com/freshdesk/
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> Helpdesk platform with AI chatbot (Freddy), auto-triage, suggested responses, and canned reply recommendations.

**Best for:** Small to mid-size teams that need a capable helpdesk without enterprise pricing. Growth at $19/agent/mo is the cheapest credible AI-equipped helpdesk of the tools here, and every tier now includes Freddy AI Agent with the first 500 sessions bundled.

**Limitations:** The free 2-agent plan has been removed — there is now only a 14-day trial, so this is no longer a zero-cost option for early-stage teams, and the entry price rose from $15 to $19/agent/mo. Freddy AI Copilot is a $29/agent/mo add-on on top of the plan, and extra AI Agent sessions cost $49 per 100. Chatbot quality is a step below Intercom Fin for autonomous resolution. Reporting and customization feel constrained compared to Zendesk at the enterprise level.

## Tidio

- **Function**: customer-service
- **Also useful for**: sales, marketing
- **Pricing**: Freemium (free tier available, $24.17/mo for Starter annual, Growth from $49.17/mo)
- **URL**: https://www.tidio.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> Live chat and AI chatbot platform designed for small business websites, with visual chatbot builder and visitor tracking.

**Best for:** Small businesses and e-commerce sites that want a chat widget with basic AI without a complex setup. The free tier survives and includes live chat plus 50 billable conversations. Visual drag-and-drop bot builder makes it accessible for non-technical users. Good for lead capture and simple FAQ automation.

**Limitations:** The Communicator plan no longer exists — the entry paid tier is now Starter. Pricing is metered on billable conversations, not seats, so costs move with traffic rather than headcount. The Lyro AI agent is capped separately (50 one-off conversations on Starter) and is sold standalone from $32.50/mo. Less sophisticated than purpose-built support platforms for complex ticket workflows. Primarily useful for website chat — not a full omnichannel support solution.

## Ada

- **Function**: customer-service
- **Also useful for**: operations
- **Pricing**: Paid (enterprise pricing, not publicly listed)
- **URL**: https://www.ada.cx
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: medium

> No-code AI customer service automation platform that builds conversational agents trained on your company's knowledge and systems.

**Best for:** Enterprise teams handling high-volume, repetitive support across multiple channels. Ada's no-code builder lets non-engineers create and maintain AI agents that connect to CRMs, order systems, and knowledge bases. Strong in e-commerce and fintech where customers need account-specific answers (order status, billing, returns).

**Limitations:** Enterprise-only pricing puts it out of reach for small teams — no self-serve plan or public pricing, and third-party procurement data puts entry contracts in the tens of thousands of dollars per year. Billing is per conversation handled, regardless of whether the issue was resolved. Implementation requires meaningful setup time to integrate with backend systems. Performance depends heavily on the quality of your training data and knowledge base. Less community and third-party ecosystem compared to Zendesk or Intercom. Vendor site was unreachable at verification time.
