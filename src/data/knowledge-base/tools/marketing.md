# Marketing & Brand — AI Tools

## Jasper

- **Function**: marketing
- **Also useful for**: communications, sales
- **Pricing**: Paid ($69/mo for Pro, $59/mo billed annually)
- **URL**: https://www.jasper.ai
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI content platform for marketing copy, blog posts, social media, and brand-consistent writing.

**Best for:** Marketing teams and content creators who need high-volume, brand-consistent copy. Template library covers ads, emails, product descriptions, blog posts. Brand voice training keeps output on-brand.

**Limitations:** The entry-level Creator tier is gone; the cheapest plan is now Pro at $69/mo for a single seat, and adding users requires a Business quote. No free tier, only a 7-day trial. Output quality varies and needs human editing. Not great for technical or highly specialized writing.

## Canva (Magic Studio)

- **Function**: marketing
- **Also useful for**: communications, administrative, education
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($18/mo Pro, or $180/yr; Business billed per seat)
- **URL**: https://www.canva.com/pricing/
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> Design platform with AI-powered image generation, background removal, text-to-design, and brand kit management.

**Best for:** Non-designers who need professional-looking visuals. Social media posts, presentations, flyers, marketing materials. AI features handle resizing, background removal, and style suggestions.

**Limitations:** Pro has risen twice since 2025 ($12.99 to $15 to $18/mo), with annual billing at $180/yr. The flat-rate Teams plan is now per-seat Canva Business, so team costs scale linearly with headcount and there is no published volume discount; published Business rates vary by source and region, so quote it from the checkout, not from a tracker. Canva has committed to 60 days' notice before future increases. The free tier remains usable. Templates can look generic, and advanced design still requires Figma or Adobe. Pro price confirmed from multiple independent trackers — canva.com blocks automated fetches.

## Gamma

- **Function**: marketing
- **Also useful for**: communications, sales, administrative
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($10/mo Plus, $8/mo billed annually; Ultra $100/mo)
- **URL**: https://gamma.app/pricing
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> AI-powered presentation and document builder that generates polished decks from text prompts.

**Best for:** Creating presentations quickly without design skills. Takes text input or outlines and generates visually polished slides. Good for internal presentations, pitch decks, and reports.

**Limitations:** The free plan is not a renewable tier — it grants 400 AI credits once at signup and they do not refresh, so sustained use requires a paid plan. Paid tiers meter monthly credits (1,000 on Plus, 4,000 on Pro, 20,000 on Ultra). Limited customization compared to PowerPoint/Keynote. Templates can feel samey. Plus at $10/mo monthly is well corroborated; the intermediate Pro price is reported inconsistently across sources ($18-$25/mo), so confirm at checkout.
