# Analysis & Decision Tools

Tools for research, evaluation, strategic planning, judgment calls, and data-driven decision making.

## Perplexity AI

- **Category**: analysis-decision
- **Also useful for**: information-processing
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($20/mo Pro, $200/mo Max; Education Pro $10/mo)
- **URL**: https://www.perplexity.ai/hub/pricing
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI-powered research assistant that searches the web and synthesizes answers with citations.

**Best for:** Research-heavy roles that need current, cited information. Replaces the "open 15 tabs and read them all" workflow. Pro tier includes academic paper search and file analysis.

**Limitations:** Citations are not always perfectly matched to claims. Free tier caps Pro Searches to a handful per day. Can miss niche or specialized sources. Not a replacement for deep domain expertise. Max at $200/mo is a big step up from Pro and only worth it for sustained deep-research volume.

## Tableau (with AI)

- **Category**: analysis-decision
- **Pricing**: Paid ($75/user/mo for Creator, billed annually; $42 Explorer, $15 Viewer)
- **URL**: https://www.tableau.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> Business intelligence platform with AI-powered natural language queries and automated insights.

**Best for:** Analysts and managers who need to explore data visually. Ask Tableau questions in plain English, get charts. AI suggests relevant visualizations and highlights anomalies.

**Limitations:** Steep learning curve for advanced features. Expensive per-seat. Requires clean, well-structured data sources.

## Julius AI

- **Category**: analysis-decision
- **Also useful for**: information-processing
- **Pricing**: Freemium (free tier ~15 messages/mo; $20/mo for Plus, $45/mo for Pro)
- **URL**: https://julius.ai
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: medium

> AI data analyst that processes spreadsheets, creates visualizations, and runs statistical analyses from natural language.

**Best for:** Non-technical professionals who need data analysis without coding. Upload a CSV, ask questions, get charts and statistical summaries. Handles regression, correlation, trend analysis.

**Limitations:** Limited to tabular data. Complex multi-step analyses can produce incorrect results. Not suitable for production data pipelines.

## Notion AI

- **Category**: analysis-decision
- **Also useful for**: information-processing, coordination-management
- **Pricing**: Bundled into Notion Business ($20/member/mo); trial-only AI on Free and Plus
- **URL**: https://www.notion.com/product/ai
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> AI features built into Notion's workspace: summarize pages, extract action items, generate content, answer questions across your workspace.

**Best for:** Teams already using Notion for documentation. AI can search across your entire workspace to answer questions, summarize meeting notes, draft project briefs, and extract insights from existing docs.

**Limitations:** Only useful if your team's knowledge is in Notion. Quality depends heavily on how well-organized your workspace is. Add-on cost on top of existing Notion subscription.

## Power BI (with Copilot)

- **Category**: analysis-decision
- **Also useful for**: information-processing
- **Pricing**: Paid ($14/user/mo for Pro, $24.99/user/mo for Premium Per User; Copilot requires PPU or Fabric capacity)
- **URL**: https://powerbi.microsoft.com
- **Verified**: 2026-08-27
- **Confidence**: high

> Microsoft's BI platform with AI-powered natural language queries, automated report generation, and anomaly detection.

**Best for:** Organizations in the Microsoft ecosystem. Ask data questions in plain English, auto-generate reports, detect outliers. Integrates natively with Excel, Azure, and Dynamics.

**Limitations:** Copilot features require Premium capacity (expensive). DAX formula language has a steep learning curve. Best with Microsoft data sources.
