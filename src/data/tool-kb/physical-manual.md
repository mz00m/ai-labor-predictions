# Physical & Manual Tools

Tools for hands-on tasks, equipment operation, physical presence, and field work. AI augmentation in this category is primarily about planning, documentation, and decision support rather than replacing physical labor.

## Fieldwire

- **Category**: physical-manual
- **Also useful for**: coordination-management
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($39/user/mo for Pro)
- **URL**: https://www.fieldwire.com
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: medium

> Construction management platform for task tracking, plan markup, and field reporting.

**Best for:** Construction managers and field supervisors. Digital plan markup, punch lists, daily reports, and photo documentation from job sites. Reduces paperwork and improves communication between field and office.

**Limitations:** Adoption requires training field crews on mobile devices. Most valuable for commercial construction, less for small residential jobs. Requires reliable connectivity.

## Procore

- **Category**: physical-manual
- **Also useful for**: coordination-management, analysis-decision
- **Pricing**: Enterprise (custom pricing)
- **URL**: https://www.procore.com
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: medium

> Construction management platform with AI-powered risk prediction, quality tracking, and safety compliance.

**Best for:** Large construction firms managing multiple projects. AI analyzes project data to predict delays, identify safety risks, and flag quality issues before they become problems.

**Limitations:** Enterprise pricing is significant. Implementation takes months. Overkill for small contractors.

## Augmentir

- **Category**: physical-manual
- **Also useful for**: technical-specialized
- **Pricing**: Enterprise (custom pricing)
- **URL**: https://www.augmentir.com
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: medium

> AI-powered connected worker platform for manufacturing, with digital work instructions and skills management.

**Best for:** Manufacturing and maintenance operations. AI personalizes work instructions based on worker skill level, identifies training gaps, and provides real-time guidance on complex procedures.

**Limitations:** Requires significant upfront investment in deployment and content creation. Most valuable at scale (50+ workers). Needs reliable shop-floor connectivity.

## SafetyCulture (iAuditor)

- **Category**: physical-manual
- **Also useful for**: coordination-management
- **Pricing**: Freemium ($24/user/mo for Premium)
- **URL**: https://safetyculture.com
- **Verified**: 2026-04-03
- **Confidence**: high

> Inspection and audit platform with AI-powered issue detection, reporting, and compliance tracking.

**Best for:** Any role requiring regular inspections (safety, quality, compliance). Digital checklists, photo evidence, automatic report generation. AI suggests corrective actions based on inspection findings.

**Limitations:** Template setup requires initial effort. Best ROI for teams doing 10+ inspections/week. Free tier limited to 3 users.
