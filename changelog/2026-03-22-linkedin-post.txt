jobsdata.ai Weekly Update -- Week of March 15-22, 2026

The biggest week since launch: 502 commits, a brand new page, and the site's most important visualization change yet.

New this week: jobsdata.ai/occupation-exposure -- a 342-occupation treemap showing displacement risk across five research-backed dimensions. Not just "how exposed is this job to AI?" but the full picture: adoption speed, worker adaptability, demand elasticity, and whether AI complements or replaces the work. Click any occupation for the complete breakdown. Built on Karpathy's BLS dataset, scored against Eloundou, Manning/Aguirre, Bessen, and Baslandze.

The occupation-exposure page is the thesis of jobsdata.ai made interactive: exposure alone doesn't determine displacement. When you add adoption speed, adaptability, demand elasticity, and complementarity, the risk map looks very different from the headlines. Software developers score high on exposure but also high on every buffer. Administrative assistants score moderate on exposure but low on buffers. The framework makes the nuance visible.
