jobsdata.ai Weekly Update -- Week of March 8-15, 2026

This week we shipped the Job Task Visualizer -- an interactive tool that breaks 34 occupations into their component tasks and models when AI becomes cheaper than human labor for each one. Pick a job. Adjust the task mix. Watch the automation crossover dates shift. The model uses a sigmoid function against real compute costs with BLS wage data as the baseline, and it includes a 5x deployment overhead (integration, validation, monitoring, human review) that most AI cost projections ignore. The point is not prediction -- it is making the assumptions visible and adjustable.

The Task Visualizer exists because sector-level statements about AI displacement ("30% of jobs at risk") are not useful for workforce planning. What matters is the task composition of specific jobs, how fast compute costs are declining for each task type, and what the realistic deployment overhead looks like. A financial analyst and a therapist face very different automation profiles even if both show up as "white-collar professional" in the aggregate data. That granularity is what practitioners need, and it is what we are building toward.
